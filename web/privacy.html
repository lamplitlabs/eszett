2. Was auf deinem Telefon bleibt

Eszett ist so gebaut, dass die Verarbeitung deiner Lerninhalte vollständig auf dem Gerät stattfindet. Folgende Daten entstehen und bleiben ausschließlich lokal auf deinem Telefon (im geschützten Speicher der App) und werden von uns weder übertragen noch eingesehen:

  • alles, was du dem Tutor schreibst oder sprichst, seine Antworten und Korrekturen;
  • Sprachaufnahmen: die Spracherkennung läuft mit der Erkennung deines Telefons auf dem Gerät; Eszett fordert ausdrücklich die Offline-Erkennung an und speichert keine Audiodateien;
  • dein Lernfortschritt, Lernstreaks, gespeicherte Wörter, Fehlerheft, Prüfungsergebnisse, Sprechübungen;
  • das optionale Profil „Über dich“ (Name, Wohnort, Herkunft, Beruf, Hobbys, Lernziel) – es dient nur dem Gesprächspartner in der App;
  • Einstellungen, Erinnerungen (lokal geplante Benachrichtigungen, kein Push-Dienst), das heruntergeladene Modell und Wörterbuch.

Du kannst diese Daten jederzeit in der App löschen oder durch Deinstallation entfernen.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Applies to the Eszett app (Android, iOS), this website and the download service behind it. The German version above is the binding one.

In short: the language model runs on your phone. What you write and say, your progress and your profile never leave the phone. You need an account for one thing – downloading the tutor model – and for that we store your e-mail address and what you downloaded and bought. Nothing else. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

1. Controller

Lamplit Labs, owner Manish Tiwari, Am Potekamp 22, 40885 Ratingen, Germany · hello@lamplitlabs.com

2. What stays on your phone

Eszett processes your learning entirely on the device. The following data exists only locally, in the app's protected storage, and is never transmitted to or seen by us:

  • everything you write or say to the tutor, its answers and corrections;
  • speech: recognition runs with your phone's on-device recognizer (Eszett explicitly requests offline recognition) and no audio is stored;
  • your progress, streaks, saved words, mistakes notebook, exam results, speaking practice;
  • the optional "About you" profile (name, city, origin, work, hobbies, goal) – used only by the in-app conversation partner;
  • settings, reminders (locally scheduled notifications, no push service), the downloaded model and dictionary.

3. What we process, and why

3.1 Account (sign-in)

Downloading the tutor model requires an account: an e-mail address and a one-time code (no password), optionally Google or Apple sign-in. Processed: e-mail address, sign-in time, technical session data (session id, device/browser identifier, IP address) and, with Google/Apple, the identifier and e-mail they pass on. Purpose and legal basis: providing the download and your entitlement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, contract); abuse prevention (Art. 6(1)(f)). Processor: Clerk, Inc., San Francisco, USA – data is processed in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and EU Standard Contractual Clauses; see clerk.com/legal/privacy.

  • no access to your content: messages, speech and progress never reach our servers;
  • no automated decision-making or profiling under Art. 22 GDPR.

5. Recipients and transfers

Recipients are the processors named above (Clerk, Microsoft, Vercel; Hugging Face as a fallback) and the stores as independent controllers. Transfers to the USA (Clerk, Vercel, Hugging Face) rest on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 45, 46 GDPR).

6. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete the account – in the app (Me › Account › Delete account) or by writing to us; see deleting your account. Deletion removes your e-mail address at Clerk and every record on our server at once; receipts we must retain are blocked and deleted when the period ends. Data on your phone stays until you delete it.

Access (Art. 15), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction (18), portability (20) and objection to processing based on legitimate interests (21 GDPR): write to hello@lamplitlabs.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for instance the one responsible for our seat.
