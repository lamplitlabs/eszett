Eszett is a German tutor that lives on your phone. A language model trained to teach German runs on the device itself: it explains, corrects, plays the other person in everyday scenes and rates how you speak – without an internet connection, and without a word of yours leaving the phone.

WHAT YOU GET

• A course from A1 to C2: 56 weeks, each with a goal, two or three grammar cards, a word field, a scene to play, something to read and listen to, a writing task and a check. Built from the official exam syllabi.
• Ask anything: "seit" or "vor"? "als", "wenn" or "wann"? Type a sentence and watch the red pen. Explanations in your language – twelve to choose from.
• 71 grammar rules with examples and the typical mistakes, checked against verified data before you learn from them.
• 58 real conversations: the bakery, the doctor on the phone, the Bürgeramt, a flat viewing, a job interview, a parents' evening. The tutor plays the other person and corrects you at the end, never mid-flow.
• One minute of German a day: speak freely, the phone writes along, counts your words and pace, and the tutor rates you the way an examiner would.
• Mock oral exams for every level – Goethe, telc, DTZ, TestDaF, DSH – timed, with a task sheet and an examiner who asks back.
• Living in Germany: the letter from the Bürgeramt, the Nebenkostenabrechnung, the sick note, the Deutschlandticket counter – read the real letters, play the real conversations.
• Stories to read from A2 to C2: Grimm, Hebel, Storm, Kafka, Kleist – with questions and a glossary.
• Practise together: pass the phone to a friend for a tandem talk, a word duel, Taboo, a story chain.
• Streaks, a word of the day, reminders when you want them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

The model, your sentences, your progress and your profile stay on your device. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. An account is needed for one thing only: downloading the tutor model (about 2.2 GB, best over Wi‑Fi). After that, everything works on a plane.

PRICING THAT DOES NOT NAG

Everything is free for a week, including one download of the tutor. Then one purchase – no subscription: the app for good, every new tutor model for twelve months, and the last of them forever. Drills, rule cards, word lookups and the games for two stay free for good. You can also pay it forward: buy full access for someone who cannot afford it, as a code to hand on or into a pool for the next learner who asks.

WHICH PHONES

Android phones with 6 GB of RAM or more (8 GB for the larger edition); Android 13 or newer for the speaking features.
